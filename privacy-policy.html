DEAD INK Privacy Policy

Wolf Revenant Studios Effective date: 28 October 2026 Privacy and support contact: parblakeman@gmail.com

This policy describes the Android version of DEAD INK supplied by Wolf Revenant Studios. It covers the offline game and the information you choose to send when contacting us for support.

Playing the game

DEAD INK works offline. The game does not require an online account and does not send your survivor profiles, game progress or settings to Wolf Revenant Studios. The current Android version does not include advertising, developer analytics, an advertising identifier, or an integrated third-party crash-reporting service.

The game saves information on your device so that you can continue playing. This includes your chosen survivor name and character appearance, XP and skills, score and cosmetics, achievements, checkpoints, difficulty, sound and accessibility preferences. These are local game profiles, not public or server-based accounts. A fictional name or nickname is sufficient; the game does not require your real name or actual demographic information.

Save files and backups

You can choose to export a save file and later import it. Exported files can contain your survivor names, appearances, progress and preferences. The Android file picker lets you choose the destination or the file to import. The game does not automatically upload that file to us.

If you select a cloud document provider, transfer or share a backup, that action is your choice and the selected provider's practices also apply. Backups are readable JSON files, so keep them somewhere you trust and avoid sharing them publicly. Importing a backup may keep a previous-save recovery copy within the game's local storage.

Contacting us

If you email parblakeman@gmail.com, we receive your email address, message and any information or attachments you choose to include. We use that information to respond to your request, investigate problems and maintain necessary support records. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details or information that is not needed for your request.

Support email is handled through Gmail, an email service operated by Google. Google processes email under its own terms and privacy practices. We do not sell your support correspondence or use it for advertising profiles. We retain it only as long as reasonably needed to deal with the enquiry and meet applicable legal or record-keeping obligations. You may contact us to ask about or request deletion of support information we hold, subject to any information we must lawfully retain.

Keeping and deleting local information

Your game saves remain on your device until you remove them. You can delete individual survivor slots in the character menu after confirming. To remove all internal game information, including settings and any local recovery copies, clear DEAD INK's storage in Android's app settings. Uninstalling the app normally also removes its internal data. Exported backup files are separate and must be deleted from wherever you saved or shared them.

We cannot remotely access, retrieve or erase saves that remain only on your device. Keep your own backup if you want to preserve your progress before clearing storage or moving devices.

Security and other services

The Android game keeps its local data within its app storage. No method of storage or communication can be guaranteed completely secure. We do not claim that exported JSON backups are encrypted.

Google Play, Android and any email or document-provider services you choose to use operate independently under their own privacy practices. This policy describes Wolf Revenant Studios' game and support handling; it does not replace those services' policies.

Intended audience

DEAD INK is a horror game with graphic combat and is intended for an adult audience. It is not directed at children and does not ask children to provide personal information.

Future changes

Future downloadable content or features may change how the app works. If we introduce purchases, online services or a new data-handling practice, we will update this policy and the relevant store disclosures before making those changes available. The effective date above identifies this version of the policy.

Contact

For privacy questions, support or requests about information you have sent us, email parblakeman@gmail.com and identify DEAD INK in your message.
